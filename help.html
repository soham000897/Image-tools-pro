Help & Support Guide

Detailed explanations and step-by-step solutions for using Pro Image Tools.

How to Compress Image Without Losing Quality?

Follow these steps to compress your image efficiently:

  • Open the Image Compressor tool.
  • Upload your image file.
  • Adjust quality between 0.6 – 0.8 for best balance.
  • Click Compress and download the optimized image.
Tip: Lower quality reduces file size more, but too low may affect clarity.

How to Resize Image Properly?

  • Open the Image Resizer page.
  • Enter width and height in pixels.
  • Maintain aspect ratio to avoid distortion.
  • Download the resized image.

JPG vs PNG – Which Should You Use?

JPG format is ideal for photographs and smaller file sizes. PNG format supports transparency and is better for logos or graphics.

Is This Tool Safe & Private?

Yes. All image processing happens directly in your browser. No images are uploaded or stored on any server.
